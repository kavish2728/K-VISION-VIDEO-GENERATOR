How To Use K. Video Generator

1. Upload Any Photo: Tap 'Select Image From File' to load any JPG, PNG, or WEBP picture.

3. Set Zoom Depth: Adjust the zoom slider to control how far into the photo the camera travels in 5 seconds.

4. Download MP4 Video: Tap 'Download MP4 Video' to save your 5-second video file directly as an MP4.
